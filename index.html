Questions we think our guests might be wondering, hmmm.

When is the RSVP deadline?

Please respond by 30th November 2026 so we can share a final count with our venue and vendors. We’d be so grateful if you could reply by then.

What time should I arrive for the dinner reception?

Please arrive by 6:00–6:15pm. Dinner starts at 7.00pm, so a little early helps with seating and photos.

Is the food halal, and is any alcohol served?

The dinner reception food is certified halal and there will be no alcohol served through the night.

Are children welcome?

We love your little ones, and we know this is a special request. To keep the evening intimate and allow everyone to celebrate fully, we’ve chosen an adults-only wedding. We hope you can still join us and enjoy a night out.

What is the dress code?

Semi-formal would be great!

Where can out-of-town guests stay?

We recommend staying near Kuching Waterfront as there are plenty of hotels around. We’re happy to share more options if needed.

Is there parking?

Yes, parking is available near the tea house, subject to first come, first served. You may also consider Grab as an alternative option.

Will there be any transport provided?

We are providing a shuttle van for guests to return to the city centre (Waterfront area) after the dinner reception.

Whom should I contact with last-minute questions?

Please DM Michelle or Tin.
